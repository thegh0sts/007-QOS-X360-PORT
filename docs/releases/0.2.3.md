Update to the 007: Quantum of Solace (Xbox 360) PC port.

**You need your own copy of the game.** `QuantumOfSolace.exe` is the launcher and installer; it builds the game on your PC from your Xbox 360 disc image.

## Updating from 0.2.2
Download `QuantumOfSolace.exe` below and run it (or use **Check for updates** in the launcher). It updates the renderer and rebuilds the game, so the first start takes a little longer, and the first level you load builds its graphics pipelines again (a few seconds). Your saves and settings stay.

## Fixed
- **Montenegro Train: no more falling between the cars above 30 FPS.** The moves along the side of the cars dropped Bond onto the tracks at every gap at 60 FPS and up. They now work at any frame rate.
- **Montenegro Train: no more long stretches at around 10 FPS.** The renderer was re-checking the same changing data tens of thousands of times a frame there.
- **Sprint and walk keys:** on keyboard, **Shift** sprints and **Ctrl** walks, as in the PC version of the game (the prompts and the launcher's keyboard map show it).

## What's new
- **Motion blur:** Original / Off / On (**Graphics**). Original leaves it to the game, which turns it on at moments such as takedowns.
- **Damage blur** and **Bloom** can be turned off (**Graphics**): the blur when you're hurt, and the glow around bright areas.
- **90 FPS (experimental)** under **Graphics → Frame rate**, beside 120.
- **Renderer improvements** that cut work per draw on recent graphics drivers (textures are handed to the GPU more cheaply, and draws that differ only in a few settings now share one pipeline).
- **Launcher:** opens on the **Game files** tab, and the tabs have rounded corners.

## Please tell us
If anything moves strangely or gets stuck on a ledge, wall or mantle above 30 FPS, tell us where (open an issue on GitHub). The train fix changes how those moves are stepped everywhere in the game.

## Known issues
- Work in progress: 10 of the 15 missions have been played through so far.
- **Busy train and casino scenes run in the low 50s at 60 FPS** (very many objects drawn per frame), and opening the phone there gives a short hitch.
- **Some decals flicker** (stickers on walls and panels, cracked glass): they fight with the surface under them.
- At 90 and 120 FPS a few busy spots dip lower, for example the rooftop chase in the second level, where the game itself needs more time per frame.
- At render resolutions above 720p, the black bars of some video cutscenes are see-through.
- The balance beam hint shows no button.
- Some scripted moments may still get stuck above 30 FPS: set the frame rate to 30 in the launcher if a mission won't progress.
- Multiplayer has only been tested with two copies on one PC. Xbox LIVE in the multiplayer menu doesn't work yet: use System Link.
