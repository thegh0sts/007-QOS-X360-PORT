Update to the 007: Quantum of Solace (Xbox 360) PC port.

**You need your own copy of the game.** `QuantumOfSolace.exe` is the launcher and installer; it builds the game on your PC from your Xbox 360 disc image.

## Updating from 0.1.1
Download `QuantumOfSolace.exe` below and run it (or use **Check for updates** in the launcher). It updates the renderer and rebuilds the game; your saves and settings stay.

## What's new
- **Keyboard and mouse:** play with the PC game's layout (WASD, mouse look, left click fire, right click aim, E cover, Ctrl sprint, Shift walk, Q take down and more). Takedowns and lockpicking use W A S D, balancing A / D, and Esc goes back in menus.
- **Keyboard prompts:** the game's button pictures show the right keys while you play with the keyboard and mouse: on-screen hints, tutorial boxes, takedowns, menus and the phone.
- **Input device choice** (Controls tab): Auto (controllers), Keyboard and mouse, Xbox controller or PlayStation controller. Only the settings for the chosen device are shown, with a map of what each button or key does.
- **Mouse sensitivity and invert** for keyboard and mouse.
- **Render threads** (Graphics tab): 1 (original), 2 (the default, as before) or 3. 3 adds a thread that prepares textures, for fewer hitches when new areas load.
- **V-sync is now off by default**, which keeps heavy scenes smoother at 60 FPS. Turn it on in the Display tab if you see tearing.
- Renderer: faster texture handling.

## Known issues
- Work in progress: not every level has been played through yet.
- **MULTIPLAYER on the main menu closes the game** (multiplayer isn't part of this port yet). Don't choose it.
- The balance beam hint shows no button.
- Some scripted moments may get stuck above 30 FPS: set the frame rate to 30 in the launcher if a mission won't progress.
