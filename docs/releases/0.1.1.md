Update to the 007: Quantum of Solace (Xbox 360) PC port.

**You need your own copy of the game.** `QuantumOfSolace.exe` is the launcher and installer; it builds the game on your PC from your Xbox 360 disc image.

## Updating from 0.1.0
Download `QuantumOfSolace.exe` below and run it (or use **Check for updates** in the launcher). It updates the renderer and rebuilds the game; your saves and settings stay.

## What's new
- **Title screen prompts:** the Press Start screen now shows "A SELECT" and "B QUIT (HOLD)" in the main menu's style (cross and circle with a PlayStation controller). Holding B still closes the game.
- **Upscaling option** (Display tab): AMD FSR 1 or CAS for scaling the picture to your screen. FSR 1 works best with a render resolution below your screen's (for example 2560x1440 on a 4K screen).
- **Faster first start:** the shaders built at the first start now come from 6 of the game's files instead of 13 (about 3 seconds instead of 8), and the list covers more of the game (1,437 shaders).
- Renderer: a debugging aid for reporting graphics problems (no effect in normal play).

## Known issues
- Work in progress: not every level has been played through yet.
- Some scripted moments may get stuck above 30 FPS: set the frame rate to 30 in the launcher if a mission won't progress.
- Keyboard and mouse aren't supported yet.
