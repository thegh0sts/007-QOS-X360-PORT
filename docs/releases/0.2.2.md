Update to the 007: Quantum of Solace (Xbox 360) PC port.

**You need your own copy of the game.** `QuantumOfSolace.exe` is the launcher and installer; it builds the game on your PC from your Xbox 360 disc image.

## Updating from 0.2.1
Download `QuantumOfSolace.exe` below and run it (or use **Check for updates** in the launcher). It updates the renderer and rebuilds the game, so the first start takes a little longer. Your saves and settings stay.

## What's new
- **Smoother frame rate in busy scenes.** The renderer does much less work per frame: explosions and other effect-heavy moments no longer drag the frame rate down the way they did, and busy areas hold 60 FPS far more often.
- **120 FPS (experimental).** A new choice under **Graphics → Frame rate**. Quiet areas run well above 60; the busiest scenes can drop below 120. Multiplayer stays at 60.
- **Balance beams behave the same at any frame rate.** They used to get harder the higher the frame rate (already twice as twitchy at 60 as on the console, and nearly impossible with a controller at 120). They now handle as in the original game.
- **Render threads: Auto.** The launcher picks how many extra threads the renderer uses for your PC (up to four, including a new one for copying the game's changing data). You can still choose 1-4 yourself.
- **New graphics options:** **Depth of field** and **Hit markers** can be turned off, **Detail distance** keeps detailed models further away, and **Sun shadow range** makes sun shadows reach further.
- **Launcher redesign:** settings laid out in two columns with button groups, a **Credits** tab, matching scroll bars, and the Saves tab tidied up. The window keeps a fixed size and long tabs scroll.
- **Multiplayer: Host addresses** (Multiplayer tab, optional): the addresses of the PCs hosting games, for playing over Tailscale or another VPN that doesn't pass on local broadcasts. Only the players joining need it.

## Fixed
- Multiplayer no longer runs out of memory when loading a map with the DLC folder set.

## Please tell us
**Detail distance** and **Sun shadow range** are new and we'd like to hear how they look and run: what changed on screen, whether the frame rate dropped, and where in the game you noticed it. Open an issue on GitHub.

## Known issues
- Work in progress: not every level has been played through yet.
- **Some decals flicker** (stickers on walls and panels, cracked glass): they fight with the surface under them.
- **At 120 FPS, a few busy spots dip lower,** for example the rooftop chase in the second level, where the game itself (not the renderer) needs more time per frame.
- At render resolutions above 720p, the black bars of some video cutscenes are see-through.
- The balance beam hint shows no button.
- Some scripted moments may get stuck above 30 FPS: set the frame rate to 30 in the launcher if a mission won't progress.
- Multiplayer has only been tested with two copies on one PC. Xbox LIVE in the multiplayer menu doesn't work yet: use System Link.
